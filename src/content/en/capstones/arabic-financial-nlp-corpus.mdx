## Abstract

Arabic NLP research is constrained by data. Labelled financial text datasets for Egyptian Arabic do not exist in the public domain. This project builds one — 55,000 news items from six Egyptian financial news sources, annotated for named entities (companies, people, instruments), sentiment, and EGX ticker linkage.

The dataset is released under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0. Anyone can use it.

## What we built

A data pipeline that:
1. Scraped and deduplicated 140,000 raw Arabic financial news items (2020–2025)
2. Filtered to 55,000 high-quality items with clear financial content
3. Annotated a 10,000-item subset for entities, sentiment, and ticker linkage using an annotation team of 6 volunteers

The full 55,000-item corpus is available in the repository. The annotated 10,000-item subset is in a separate folder.

## Methodology

We developed annotation guidelines specific to Egyptian financial Arabic — including handling of mixed Arabic/English company names (which appear frequently in Egyptian financial media), abbreviations, and informal language used in social-media-adjacent financial commentary.

Inter-annotator agreement (Cohen's kappa) was 0.82 on sentiment, 0.91 on entity identification.

## What we'd do next

Expand the annotated subset to 30,000 items. Train and release a benchmark model on the corpus. Create an Arabic-language financial NER model specifically for Egyptian company names and their common variants.
