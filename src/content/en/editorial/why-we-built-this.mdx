EFG Hermes has operated in Egyptian capital markets for over 40 years. In that time, we've built significant infrastructure: data systems, analytical tools, research workflows. Most of it has been internal.

The Innovation Hub is an acknowledgment that this infrastructure is more valuable when it's shared.

Capital markets infrastructure is not charity. It's the foundation that allows a market to function — the price data, the analytical frameworks, the programming tools that let people ask questions and get answers. When that infrastructure is locked inside institutions, the market is less efficient, less accessible, and less interesting than it could be.

We built the Foundation Series because the first barrier to participation in Egyptian capital markets is not money — it's conceptual. Most people don't have a clear mental model of what an exchange actually does, what a limit order is, or how to read a balance sheet. Five sessions won't make someone a professional analyst, but they'll give someone the vocabulary to start.

We built the API because the second barrier is data. Good analysis requires good data. The EFG API makes EGX market data available to anyone who wants to build on it.

We built the showcase because the third barrier is visibility. Brilliant work gets done by students and independent developers in Egypt every year. Most of it disappears. The showcase is a permanent record of that work.

None of this is altruism. An ecosystem where more people understand markets, build on market data, and compete transparently is better for EFG and better for Egypt. These are aligned.
