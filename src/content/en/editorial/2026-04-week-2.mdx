CIB was the week's standout: up 3.2% on volume that ran double the 20-day average. No specific announcement — the move looked like institutional accumulation ahead of the quarterly earnings release due in two weeks.

EAST (Eastern Tobacco) was flat on thin volume. SWDY pulled back 1.4% after a three-week run that took it 11% above its 200-day moving average.

The EGX 30 closed up 1.1% for the week. Total traded value was EGP 3.8 billion, slightly above the recent monthly average. No circuit breakers triggered.

## One thing worth watching

The spread between 10-year EGP T-bills and the EGX 30 earnings yield has narrowed to its tightest level since early 2024. When bonds yield nearly as much as equities, some institutional money rotates out of equities into fixed income. Worth watching over the next few weeks.
